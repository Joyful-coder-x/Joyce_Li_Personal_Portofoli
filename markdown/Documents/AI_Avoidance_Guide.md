# AI Avoidance Guide

This document lists directions and styles that should not be pursued in further AI‑assisted iterations of this project.

## Do Not Generate
- New assets that diverge from the established handcrafted/sketch aesthetic unless explicitly requested.
- Overly commercial or corporate styles (e.g., glassmorphism, heavy gradients, glowing neon).
- Complex 3D floating UI elements or flashy animations that distract from content.
- Highly polished, generic templates that feel impersonal or out of context.
- Mixed aesthetics from unrelated domains without a clear concept.
- Cluttered layouts with excessive text or decorative elements.
- Overly modern corporate color schemes that contradict the warm, organic palette.

## Avoid Repetition
- Re‑creating assets that are already finalized and approved.
- Changing the core style (handcrafted, organic) into something entirely new.
- Overloading pages with icons and patterns without clear purpose.

## Favor
- Simple, organic shapes and textures.
- Cohesive, unified colour palette.
- Maintaining clarity and readability.
